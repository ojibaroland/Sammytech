Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your services cost?

Every job is different, so we quote per job after understanding the work involved. Send us a description (and photos if possible) on WhatsApp or through the quote form, and we'll respond with a clear price. Site inspection for larger installations is free within Enugu.

Do you handle emergency repairs?

Yes. For burst pipes, major leaks or flooding, call 0701 251 8327 directly — we prioritise emergencies and offer same-day response within Enugu.

Do you supply the materials or do I buy them myself?

Either works. We can supply quality pipes and fittings at market price (with receipts), or work with materials you've already bought. Most clients prefer we handle it so everything is compatible and guaranteed.

Do you work on new buildings as well as repairs?

Yes — we do full plumbing for new construction (carcass piping through to final fixtures), renovations, and one-off repairs on existing buildings.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Any fault arising from our workmanship is corrected free of charge. We build our business on referrals, so we don't leave jobs half-done.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer, whichever is convenient. For larger installations we agree a simple payment structure upfront — typically part payment to start and balance on completion after your inspection.
